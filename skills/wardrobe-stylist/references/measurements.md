# Measurements and photos

Ask for the dimensions relevant to the requested garments, not a full questionnaire every time. Use confirmed conversation data and keep body and garment measurements separate. A well-fitting existing garment often gives better shopping criteria than a size label.

## Body measurements

Measure in centimetres with a flexible tape, relaxed posture and normal breathing. Keep the tape level, snug without compressing, over thin clothing. Another person helps with shoulders and sleeves.

| ID | How to measure | Useful for |
|---|---|---|
| `height` | Barefoot against a wall, crown to floor. | General length and specialist-store routing |
| `chest` | Around the fullest chest and shoulder blades, arms relaxed. | Tops and outer layers |
| `waist` | Around the exact level where the intended waistband sits; record that level. | Trousers/skirts; differs from natural waist |
| `hips` | Around the fullest seat, feet together. | Trousers/skirts/dresses |
| `shoulders` | Across the back between the intended shoulder seam points; note if approximate. | Structured tops/outerwear |
| `neck` | Around the base of the neck; record body circumference, not collar size. | Buttoned shirt collars |
| `foot_length` | Standing with heel at a wall, longest toe distance; both feet, use the larger. | Optional shoe refinement |

Weight is contextual and optional. Do not calculate dimensions or diagnose body shape from weight/photos.

## Reference garment measurements

Lay a well-fitting garment flat without stretching; fasten as worn. Record the garment type and measurement endpoints.

| ID | How to measure | Do not confuse with |
|---|---|---|
| `inseam` | Along inside leg seam, crotch intersection to hem. | Outside leg or nominal L label |
| `sleeve_outer` | Shoulder seam to cuff along the outer sleeve. | Neck-centre-to-cuff / raglan sleeve |
| `sleeve_inner` | Underarm seam to cuff. | Outer sleeve |
| `top_length` | Highest shoulder point beside neckline to hem. | Back-centre / collar-inclusive length |
| `shoulders` | Shoulder seam to shoulder seam across back. | Body shoulder width with different endpoints |
| `chest`, `waist`, `hips` | Flat width at the specified level; mark `flat_width` explicitly. | A full circumference |

Do not automatically double a width unless it is explicitly measured flat across the relevant garment. Body chest versus garment chest is not a same-basis comparison; the agent must choose appropriate ease for fabric, cut and layering. A size chart may describe recommended body dimensions instead of the actual garment.

## Photos

Request a clear face photo and a full-body front view in simple clothes that show the silhouette without being tight. Use even daylight, a plain background, normal posture and unobstructed shoulders/waist/legs. Include head and shoes with margin. Put the camera straight and sufficiently far away to avoid wide-angle distortion; avoid tilted close-up shots. A side view is helpful but optional. A mirror selfie is acceptable when no better photo exists; explain that the phone and perspective may obscure proportions.

Do not request undressing. Do not estimate exact circumferences from a photograph or apply unrequested body changes in renders. Identity photos are not product references; retailer model photos are not identity references.

## Handling gaps

Show the known table first, then ask a small relevant batch with a measuring method for each missing value. Explain why endpoints matter when a value looks unusual. Keep a confirmed unusual value and flag uncertainty rather than replacing it with a typical value.

If the user explicitly chooses nominal-size shoe shopping, accept that policy and mark fit provisional. If photos are missing, research clothing while asking for photos; do not invent a likeness. If measurements are incomplete, present approximate candidates and identify the dimensions needed before choosing a final size.
